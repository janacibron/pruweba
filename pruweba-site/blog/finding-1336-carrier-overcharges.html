Finding $1,336 in Carrier Overcharges

By Jan Michael Acibron · Pruweba

What this is

This is a representative output from a live reconciliation run in the Pruweba proof-of-work demo. It shows how the system processes real carrier invoices and flags a concrete variance against contract rates.

The run

The demo ingests multiple invoices and BOLs, validates document structure, checks for duplicates, and reconciles line items against expected rates. The output below is from the actual reconciliation panel on the homepage.

[FLAG] XXV ExplainabilityEngine: carrier overcharging by $1,336.00 vs contract rate

That flag is not a black-box alert. It is produced by the Explainability Engine with the specific variance, the contract rate, and the invoice source.

Which pillars are involved

  • XI FederationEngine — ingests invoices and BOLs from multiple sources
  • XXI SchemaValidator — checks SCAC and document structure
  • XXII IdempotencyGuard — drops duplicate BOL references
  • XXV ExplainabilityEngine — produces the explainable variance flag
  • XXIX DeadLetterHandler — routes incomplete documents to human review
  • XIV LineageNotary — records the decision trail

Why this matters

Carrier overcharges are common, repeatable, and usually small enough that manual review doesn't happen. A system that flags the exact variance, source document, and contract rate turns leakage into a recoverable event instead of background noise.
