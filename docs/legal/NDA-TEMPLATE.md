a) Use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose.

e) Return or destroy all Confidential Information upon request.

## 4. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that:

a) Is or becomes publicly available through no fault of Receiving Party.
b) Was rightfully known to Receiving Party prior to disclosure.
c) Is independently developed by Receiving Party without use of Confidential Information.
d) Is required to be disclosed by law (with prior notice to Disclosing Party).

## 5. Term

This NDA is effective from the date above and continues for 3 years from the last disclosure of Confidential Information.
